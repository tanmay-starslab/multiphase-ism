How to read

  • Rows are lines, cool to hot. Columns are instrument cases (Layout).
  • Flux is normalised to the continuum; v is the line-of-sight velocity relative to the mixing-layer or stratified-ISM rest frame (vsys = 0 km/s).
  • Stepped line: the degraded spectrum, a Gaussian line-spread function of FWHM c/R with 5 pixels and the given S/N per resolution element. Shading: the noiseless model. Intrinsic (dashed): the noiseless spectrum before the line-spread function.
  • Fitted components: the fitted model in ink, each component in the line colour and a tick at its centre. A hollow tick marks a component on a bound, cut by the time cap, or unconstrained (log N error above 1 dex). An arrow marks a saturated component: its log N is only a lower limit, read as “log N > 14.5 sat”. The readout gives the fitted values, their errors and flags.
  • Drag or use the arrow keys to pan; ctrl or cmd with the wheel, or + and −, zoom; Home resets.
  • 3D: drag to orbit the 5-level octree volume (31 AU to 1 kpc), scroll to zoom across fractal KHI eddies, cloud-crushing tails, STARFORGE GMC filaments, superbubble shells, and cstcool mist cloudlets, click a sightline to open its 12-ion spectra. Orientation presets align along Normal (+z) (across the mixing interface / stratified midplane), In-plane (xy) (along the shear layer / ISM midplane), or Oblique 3D.

Data and methods

Sightlines through the 50 pc → 157 AU Radiative Turbulent Mixing Layer volume (Volume RTML_50pc, RTML-Athena++, 5-level L0–L4 & S0–S4 octree down to 0.00076 pc / 157 AU cells), labelled by geometry (Normal (+z) across the KHI cooling interface, In-plane (xy) along the shear layer, or Oblique 3D), impact parameter b and azimuth Φ.

Optical depths from Trident + Voigt synthesis on a 0.25 km/s grid, stored at 1 km/s over ±1500 km/s. Velocities are relative to the simulation volume rest frame.

Six flagship ISM & sub-ISM simulation regimes (31 AU → 1 kpc, 720 sightlines): RTML_50pc (50 pc → 157 AU KHI + cooling mixing layer), CloudCrush_200pc (200 pc → 630 AU radiative cloud crushing & cometary wake), GMC_STARFORGE_20pc (20 pc → 62 AU magnetized GMC & blister H II bubble), Superbubble_500pc (500 pc → 1570 AU clustered-SN X-ray superbubble breakout), ShatteringMist_10pc (10 pc → 31 AU isobaric thermal instability cstcool cloudlet mist), and ISM_1kpc (1 kpc → 3148 AU stratified multiphase ISM fountain). H I from self-shielded neutral hydrogen fraction; 11 metal ions (Mg II, Si II, C II, Si III, C III, Si IV, C IV, N V, O VI, Ne VIII, O VII) from Trident/CLOUDY photo+collisional ionization tables.

Atomic data follow Trident 1.4.2 (O VI 1031.912 Å and C IV 1548.187 Å, 3 to 4 km/s from Morton 2003); O VII from Verner et al. 1996. Damped H I wings use Trident's Γ = 4.69 × 10⁸ s⁻¹ against the standard damping constant 6.265 × 10⁸ (Morton 2003), so damped log N fitted here sits about 0.13 dex above VPFIT-style fits of the same wings. Saturated components are lower limits.

The degradation and the Voigt fits run in your browser: Levenberg–Marquardt on (log N, b, v) through the line-spread function, seeded from the intrinsic component catalogue and pruned by ΔBIC. Noise matches the publication figure only statistically.

3D & Bring-Your-Own-Simulation CLI: every volume is deposited into five nested 256³ gas/ion/velocity octree levels (L0..L4) and five nested 256³ RGBA starlight/dust levels (S0..S4) spanning 31 AU (ShatteringMist_10pc L4) to 1 kpc (ISM_1kpc L0), streamed from Hugging Face tanmay-starslab/ism-atlas-3d. 3D velocities are mass-weighted means per voxel in the volume's rest frame (vx, vy and vz each clipped at the top of the 3D velocity window). You can ingest your own Athena++, Cholla, STARFORGE, FLASH, Pluto, ENZO, RAMSES, AREPO, GIZMO, or yt-compatible HDF5/NumPy simulation into this exact 5-level 3D + 12-ion web explorer with a single command: python -m tngsight.ingest --input my_sim.h5 --out docs/ism/data --half-pc 25 --levels L0,L1,L2,L3,L4 --nrays 120.

Data and credits

Simulations & Multiphase Physics (6 Regimes, 31 AU → 1 kpc)

RTML_50pc (50 pc → 157 AU Radiative Turbulent Mixing Layer, RTML-Athena++): 3D compressible MHD radiative turbulent mixing layer resolving the fractal corrugated cooling interface where shear-driven Kelvin–Helmholtz eddies mix hot (T ~ 10⁶ K) and cold (T ~ 10²–10⁴ K) gas into intermediate-temperature (T ~ 10⁴.⁵–10⁵.⁵ K) C IV, Si IV, N V, and O VI absorbers (Fielding et al. 2020, ApJL 894, L24; Tan, Oh & Gronke 2021, MNRAS 502, 3179; Ji, Oh & Masterson 2019, MNRAS 487, 737).

CloudCrush_200pc (200 pc → 630 AU Radiative Cloud Crushing, Gronke-Oh / Cholla): Radiative cold cloud (Rcl = 10 pc, T = 10⁴ K, nH = 1.5 cm⁻³) entrained and shattering in a supersonic hot galactic wind (T = 3 × 10⁶ K, vwind = 350 km/s) with a 200 pc cometary cooling tail and Kelvin–Helmholtz mixing sheath (Gronke & Oh 2018, MNRAS 480, L111; Gronke & Oh 2020, MNRAS 492, 1970; Schneider et al. 2020 Cholla, ApJ 895, 43; Abruzzo et al. 2022, ApJ 925, 199).

GMC_STARFORGE_20pc (20 pc → 62 AU Magnetized GMC & H II Blister, STARFORGE / SILCC): Magnetized supersonic turbulent Giant Molecular Cloud filament network with an expanding blister H II photoionization and OB stellar-wind feedback cavity (Grudić et al. 2021 STARFORGE, MNRAS 506, 2199; Grudić et al. 2022, MNRAS 512, 216; Walch et al. 2015 SILCC, MNRAS 454, 238).

Superbubble_500pc (500 pc → 1570 AU Clustered-SN Superbubble, TIGRESS / PRFM): Clustered-supernova superbubble carving a hot X-ray (T ~ 3 × 10⁶–10⁷ K) chimney breakout through a stratified cold/warm H I disk with swept-up radiative shells and fragmented wall pillars (Kim, Ostriker & Raileanu 2017, ApJ 834, 25; Fielding, Quataert & Martizzi 2018, MNRAS 481, 3325; Orr et al. 2022, MNRAS 511, 6160).

ShatteringMist_10pc (10 pc → 31 AU Thermal Instability Mist, McCourt-Gronke): Isobaric thermally unstable cooling mist of thousands of coagulating r ~ cstcool ~ 100–500 AU cold cloudlets (T = 10⁴ K, nH = 3 cm⁻³) suspended in a transonic warm-hot 4 × 10⁵ K medium (McCourt et al. 2018, MNRAS 473, 5407; Gronke & Oh 2020b, MNRAS 494, L27; Waters & Proga 2019, ApJ 875, 158).

ISM_1kpc (1 kpc → 3148 AU Stratified Multiphase Interstellar Medium, TIGRESS-ISM): Stratified galactic disk patch with supernova-driven superbubble chimneys, galactic fountain outflows, warm neutral/ionized layers (WNM/WIM), and cold neutral/molecular cores (Kim & Ostriker 2017, ApJ 846, 133; Kim et al. 2020, ApJ 900, 61).

Spectra & Ionization

12-ion UV, EUV, and X-ray absorption sightline libraries (120 sightlines per volume, 720 total across Normal (+z), In-plane (xy), and Oblique 3D geometries) and browser-cast rays: Trident (Hummels, Smith & Silvia 2017, ApJ 847, 59) on yt (Turk et al. 2011, ApJS 192, 9) with CLOUDY (Ferland et al. 2017) photo+collisional ionization tables under the HM2012 radiation background (Haardt & Madau 2012, ApJ 746, 125) and self-shielded neutral hydrogen.
